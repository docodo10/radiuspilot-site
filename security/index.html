Security

Built to be safe

Your workspaces stay on your computer. Your website account keeps only what support needs. Here is exactly what goes where.

The app

Your data stays on your PC

Pitchable is a Windows app. Your work lives in one folder on your computer, with backup and restore in Settings.

  • On your computer

    Workspaces, businesses, emails and settings stay on your PC. They never go to a Pitchable server.

  • Secrets, encrypted

    Your app password is a salted hash. Mailbox and AI sign-ins are encrypted by Windows and never logged.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no tracking pixels and no crash reports, in the app or on this website.

Only what support needs

Your name and email from Google, your company, your tickets and your account activity. Export it or delete it any time.

  • Hosted in Canada

    On Supabase, in the ca-central-1 region.

  • Your rows only

    Row-level security on every table. You see only your own data; support staff see your tickets to answer them.

  • Guarded forms

    Rate limits on public forms, keyed to a salted hash, never your IP address.

Good to know

The limits, plainly

The app’s account lives on your PC

Anyone who can use your Windows account can open the app and its data. Lock your computer when you step away.

AI runs on your own account

AI is optional. Its requests go to OpenAI, outside Québec, through Codex, your ChatGPT sign-in or your API key. Do a Law 25 assessment before you send clients’ personal information.

Cloud sync is planned

Workspaces will move to the server in Canada, each one walled off. Before client data is stored there, a person in charge of personal information will be named, as Québec’s Law 25 requires.

This website

No cookies, no analytics. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits for security. The live demo and a signed-in session keep their data in your browser, on your device.
