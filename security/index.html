Security

Your data stays on your computer

RadiusPilot is a Windows app with no server behind it yet. Here’s where your data lives, how passwords are kept, and exactly what leaves your computer.

On your computer

Workspaces, businesses, emails and settings are stored by the app on your PC, with backup and restore in Settings → Data. There’s no RadiusPilot server yet, so nothing is uploaded to us.

Passwords kept safe

Your account password is kept as a salted hash, never as text. Your mailbox password, ChatGPT sign-in and API key are encrypted by Windows for your Windows account, and never logged.

No tracking

No analytics, no tracking pixels and no crash reports, in the app or on this website.

Good to know

The limits, plainly

Your account lives on this computer

Anyone who can use your Windows account can open the app and its data. Lock your computer when you step away.

AI runs on your own account

AI is optional. If you set it up, requests go to OpenAI, outside Québec, through Codex, your ChatGPT sign-in or your API key, and the table above says what they carry. Do a Law 25 privacy assessment before you send clients’ personal information.

Cloud sync is coming

Accounts and workspaces will move to a server in Canada, each workspace walled off from the others. Before any client data is stored there, a person in charge of personal information will be named, as Québec’s Law 25 requires, and this page will be updated.

This website

It sets no cookies and has no analytics. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits for security under its own privacy statement. The live demo keeps its sample workspace in your browser’s storage, on your device only.

Found a security issue?

Please tell us privately before sharing it, with the steps to see it. Our contact address is coming soon; the contact page will have it.
