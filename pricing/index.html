The rules

Questions

About plans, credits and billing

Not yet. RadiusPilot doesn’t take payments today. In the app, choosing a plan or buying credits is simulated: you get the plan’s limits and credits on your computer and a simulated invoice marked “no payment was taken”. No card is charged.

US dollars, per month, before taxes. Yearly billing takes 17% off the monthly price.

The one unit for what costs RadiusPilot money each time it runs: Verify listing, RadiusPilot AI, scans past your plan’s and the API. Most tasks use 1 to 3 credits, a scan 10. Scans within your plan, imports, exports and the tools use none.

The action stops before it starts and says so, with two ways out: buy credits, or, for AI, connect your own AI in Settings › AI. Nothing is charged automatically, and nothing pops up in the background.

Your plan’s credits renew on its renewal date and don’t roll over. Credits you buy last 12 months. Your plan’s credits are spent first, then bought ones, oldest first, and a failed action gives its credits back.

Only for light use. Pay as you go costs $0.22 to $0.29 a credit, top-ups $0.09 to $0.12. Past about 400 credits a month, Pro costs less: its 25 scans and 300 credits are worth 550 credits, about $137 pay as you go, for $99.

Each real scan of one area counts as one, whatever the number of kinds of businesses, and scanning an area again is a new scan. A scan that fails or finds nothing gives its scan back, and Starter’s preview scan isn’t used up, so you can try again. Past your plan’s scans, a scan uses 10 credits.

How many real businesses it found, how many are high opportunity, and up to three of them. Their phone numbers and websites, and the rest of the list, open on a paid plan or with a scan paid in credits.

Yes. Pro comes with 25, 50 or 100 scans and 300, 600 or 1,200 credits a month, Agency with 100, 250 or 500 scans and 1,000, 2,000 or 4,000 credits. You choose the volume with the plan.

Five things: the real scans a month, the businesses per scan, the credits it includes and the price of more, the seats, and white-label on Agency. Nothing else is counted or locked by plan today: reports, pitch decks, lead lists, PDF export, the pipeline, clients and projects work on every plan.

The website audit and the eight tools are part of every plan and use no credits. RadiusPilot AI is included, in the web app and the Windows app: each task uses 1 to 5 credits. AI of your own uses none: in the Windows app, Codex or Continue with ChatGPT use your ChatGPT plan, and an OpenAI API key is billed by OpenAI. RadiusPilot charges nothing for either.

Yes, in beta. The RadiusPilot API searches an area’s businesses with their scores, checks a website and drafts outreach with RadiusPilot AI, billed in the same credits. It opens to accepted beta testers first, each with 50 credits to try it.

“Planned” is announced in the app’s plan sheet, where it says Soon, and not built yet: competitor intelligence, CRM sync, custom domains, priority scanning and webhooks. Each one moves to the changelog when it ships. “Beta” is the website builder and its editor, and the API: open to beta testers first, on any plan.
