About plans and billing

Not yet. Pitchable doesn’t take payments today. In the app, choosing a plan is simulated: you get the plan’s limits on your computer and a simulated invoice marked “no payment was taken”. No card is charged.

US dollars, per month. Yearly billing takes 17% off the monthly price.

Each scan of one area counts as one, real or demo. Starter’s real scan isn’t used up if it fails or finds nothing, so you can try again.

How many real businesses it found, how many are high opportunity, and up to three of them. Their phone numbers and websites, and the rest of the list, open on a paid plan.

Yes. Pro comes with 25, 50 or 100 scans a month, Agency with 100, 250 or 500. You choose the volume with the plan.

No. The website audit and the eight tools are part of every plan. AI is optional and runs on your own account in the Windows app: Codex or Continue with ChatGPT use your ChatGPT plan, and an OpenAI API key is billed by OpenAI. Pitchable charges nothing for either.

Announced in the app’s plan sheet, not built yet: CRM sync, custom domains, priority scanning and the API. Each one moves to the changelog when it ships.
