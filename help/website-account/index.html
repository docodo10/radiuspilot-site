Account and data

Your Pitchable account

Sign in on this website with Google, keep your account secure, follow support tickets, and export or delete your data.

Updated October 27, 2026

On this page
  1. Sign in

  3. Profile and email

Your Pitchable account lives on this website. Use it to get the app, follow your support tickets and manage your sign-in. The Windows app keeps its own account on your computer, separate from this one.

Sign in

  1. 1

    Continue with Google

    On the sign-in page, choose Continue with Google, then pick your Google account. Your first sign-in creates your Pitchable account.

  2. 2

    Confirm with Google

    Google asks you to share your name, email address and profile picture with Pitchable. We never see your Google password.

  3. 3

    Your account opens

    You land on your account page. If two-step verification is on, type the code from your authenticator app first.

Keep it secure

Turn on Two-step verification in Settings. Scan the code with your authenticator app, or type its key by hand, then enter the 6-digit code it shows. From then on, signing in also asks for a code from the app, after Google.

Sign out other devices ends every session but the one you’re using. Your Google account’s own security, such as its password and 2-Step Verification, protects your sign-in too.

Profile and email

Change your name, your company and Product news by email in Settings. Your email address is the one of the Google account you sign in with. To use another address, sign in with that Google account.

Support tickets

Choose New ticket in your account or on the Support page, pick a topic and say what happened. Our replies arrive in the same thread. Mark as solved when you’re done: replying opens it again. Never send a password in a ticket.

Your data

Export my data downloads everything stored about your account as one file, including your email and sign-in dates, your profile, your tickets and messages, and your account activity.

Delete my account removes your account, your profile, your tickets and their messages, and your account activity. Type DELETE to confirm. It needs a sign-in from the last 15 minutes (choose Sign in again, then Continue with Google), and a code from your app if two-step verification is on.

Deleting your website account doesn’t touch the Windows app: its data stays on your computer. The privacy policy says what’s stored, where and for how long.

Still stuck?

Write to us from the Support page. Tell us what you tried and what happened.
