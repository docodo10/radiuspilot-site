From the first scan to the signed client

RadiusPilot finds real businesses around any address, shows what each one is missing, and gives you what you need to pitch, build and follow up. One Windows app, with your data on your computer.

ScopeScan and ScopeMap

Real businesses, around any address

Pick a place, a radius and the kinds of businesses you sell to. RadiusPilot asks OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can edit, for every named business that matches.

  • Search any address or neighbourhood, then pick a radius from 0.5 to 5 km
  • 18 kinds of local businesses, from dentists and cafés to roofers and law firms
  • Up to 500 businesses per scan on paid plans, 100 on the free plan
  • ScopeMap puts every business on a map, coloured by opportunity and grouped when you zoom out
  • Sample markets let you try every screen before your first real scan

Website checks and opportunities

A score you can explain to the owner

Each business gets an opportunity score from the facts found, and the reasons sit next to it. No black box: you see why, not just a number.

  • The Windows app checks up to 300 homepages per scan: HTTPS, mobile layout, booking links, load time and the copyright year
  • High, Medium or Low, with the facts behind every point
  • OpenStreetMap has no ratings or reviews, so they never count and are never made up
  • Opportunities ranks the businesses most worth a call

Mail, sequences and offers

Outreach that follows the rules

Write from your own mailbox, plan the follow-ups and keep every reply in one place. The rules that keep you out of trouble are built in, not optional.

  • Connect Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365, iCloud, Yahoo, Zoho, Fastmail or any IMAP and SMTP mailbox in the Windows app
  • Sequences of emails, calls and tasks by day. They stop when someone replies, and nothing goes out without you
  • Every outreach email gets your mailing address and an unsubscribe line; a daily limit and an opt-out list do the rest
  • An email builder, offers with payment options, and proposals to print or save as PDF
  • Today lines up your calls and emails; Call mode gives tap-to-call links and never places or records calls

AI Builder and reports

A draft before the first call

AI Builder turns what a scan found into a first draft: a website, a pitch deck, a market report or a lead list. Today the drafts come from templates filled in with each business’s facts; AI-written copy is on the roadmap.

  • Website drafts in English or French, exported as one HTML file
  • Market reports with up to nine sections, from the executive summary to the recommendations, saved as PDF
  • White-label reports and sites on the Agency plan: your name, initials and colour, without “Made with RadiusPilot”
  • Drafts are labelled as generated, so nobody mistakes them for finished work

Pipeline, tasks and analytics

Every lead, from new to won

Move each business through your pipeline, keep tasks, clients and projects together, and see which emails get replies.

  • A pipeline from New lead to Researching, Contacted, Proposal sent, Won or Lost
  • Tasks with due dates in plain words, clients with their retainer, projects from planning to delivered
  • Analytics over 7, 30 or 90 days: emails sent, reply rate, meetings booked, proposals sent and revenue won
  • The best time to send, the time to first reply and results by score band

Your workspace

Built for a team, kept on your computer

Six languages

How each tool works

What’s real, and what’s sample

Real scans use OpenStreetMap, and every sample market, sample business and simulated payment is labelled in the app. Competitors, demand and gap zones need data OpenStreetMap doesn’t have, so for now they only appear in sample markets.

Sample datais the label on every invented business, number and message, here and in the app.
