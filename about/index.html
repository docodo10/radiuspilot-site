About

The best local clients are the ones missing what you sell

RadiusPilot finds them around any address, with free and open data, and helps agencies show them exactly what’s missing.

Why RadiusPilot exists

Most local businesses don’t need to be convinced that they need marketing. They need someone to show them what’s missing: a dentist with no online booking, a café whose site won’t open on a phone, a roofer with no website at all.

Those are reasons to call. RadiusPilot finds them street by street, explains each one with the facts behind it, and turns it into outreach, a draft and a follow-up.

It starts from free, open data: OpenStreetMap for the businesses and public services for the checks. Where better data costs money, like Google’s ratings and reviews, it will come from the official source and say so.

The name

A radius around a place, and a pilot for the outreach that follows. One word, with a capital R and P.

Where it stands

RadiusPilot 1.0 is getting ready for its first release, as a Windows app. A test version is out, not yet tried on a real Windows computer. Cloud accounts and a web version come next.

What we hold ourselves to

Six rules, in the app and on this site

Real, or labelled

Every sample business, number and message says Sample. Simulated sends and simulated billing say they’re simulated.

Nothing made up

No invented ratings, reviews, rankings or testimonials. When a fact isn’t known, it stays empty.

Your data, your computer

No server yet, no tracking, and passwords kept the way Windows keeps its own.

Respect the inbox

An unsubscribe line on every outreach email, a daily limit, an opt-out list, and no guessed or harvested addresses.

Free first

Open data and free public services, so an agency can start without a budget or an API key. AI is optional, on your own account.

Built for agencies

From the first scan to the signed client in one app, made for the way agencies sell.
