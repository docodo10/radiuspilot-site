Tools

Know the website before you call

A website audit, five checks and three makers, built into RadiusPilot. Included in every plan, with nothing to sign up for.

Download for WindowsSee the tools

Check

Check a website

Five checks that turn a homepage into facts for the first call. The website audit runs four of them at once.

Site speed

Google’s speed, accessibility and SEO scores for any website, on a phone or a computer.

Google PageSpeed

  • Four Lighthouse scores
  • Lab timings, and real-visitor data when Google has it
  • Google’s list of fixes and a screenshot
  • About 15 to 40 seconds a check

Email health

The records that keep a domain’s email out of spam and stop others faking it.

Public DNS

  • MX: who handles the mail
  • SPF: the senders allowed, the lookups used
  • DKIM: 22 common selectors, unknown when not found
  • DMARC: the policy, with starter records to suggest

Site technology

The builder, booking tool, analytics and plugins behind a homepage, and what’s out of date.

Reads the homepageWindows app

  • About 130 technologies, each with its clue
  • Old analytics, jQuery, PHP or WordPress
  • No HTTPS, no mobile viewport, an old copyright year
  • Header and cookie names only, never their values

Published contacts

The emails, phones, address, form and social links a business publishes on its own site, each with its page.

Reads the business’s siteWindows app

  • The homepage and up to five contact, team, about or legal pages
  • Role or personal-looking addresses, named only when the site names them
  • A page that refuses unsolicited messages blocks saving
  • Saves one role address of the site’s own domain, with its page

It never guesses addresses, never decodes the ones a site hides from robots and never searches other websites.

Site history

When the Internet Archive first saved a website, and when its homepage last changed.

Internet Archive

  • First and last saved copies
  • The last real change
  • Changes per year
  • A link to each year’s last copy

Make

Make something

Three makers for the leave-behind: a QR code, a brand kit, a social post.

QR codes

QR codes for a website, a phone, an email or Wi-Fi, in the business’s colours.

On your computer

  • Website, phone, email, Wi-Fi or text
  • Square, rounded or dot styles, with a logo
  • A warning when the contrast is too low
  • PNG or SVG; the Wi-Fi password is never saved

Brand kit

A business’s colours, readable text pairs and fonts, from its logo or its website.

On your computer

  • From a logo file, or the site’s own styles in the Windows app
  • Text and background pairs that pass WCAG contrast
  • The site’s fonts, or a pairing suggested for the business
  • CSS and design tokens (JSON); saved on the business, it colours its posts and QR codes

Post maker

Social posts filled from a business’s details and brand kit, sized for Instagram, Stories and Facebook.

On your computer

  • Announcement, offer, hours, event and tip layouts
  • Square, portrait, Story and wide sizes, saved as PNG
  • Fills in a business’s name, contact, hours and brand kit
  • Write with AI in the Windows app, labelled AI-generated

Website audit

In Tools, or Run audit in a business’s profile: six checks on one website at once, graded from A to F.

  • Google’s speed test on a phone and on a computer, Email health, Site technology, Site history and a read of the site itself
  • A grade from A to F, with a score for each section: speed, SEO, security, mobile, email and more
  • Fixes and pitch lines built only from what was found
  • A whole scan in the background, two sites at a time
  • The read of the site and Site technology need the Windows app
