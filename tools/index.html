Analyse

Check a website

Five checks that turn a homepage into facts you can bring to the first call.

Site speed

Google’s speed, accessibility and SEO scores for any website, on a phone and on a computer.

Google PageSpeedFree

  • Four Lighthouse scores: performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO
  • Lab timings, plus real-visitor data when Google has it for the site
  • Google’s list of fixes, and the final screenshot of the page
  • Loaded as a phone or as a computer would, in 15 to 40 seconds

Show a business how its homepage loads on a phone, with Google’s own numbers.

Email health

Checks the records that keep a domain’s email out of spam and stop others faking it.

Public DNSFree

  • MX: who handles the domain’s mail
  • SPF: the senders allowed, the lookups used
  • DKIM: 22 common selectors (not found means unknown, not missing)
  • DMARC: the policy and where reports go, with starter records to suggest

Nothing is sent: the check only reads public DNS records.

Site technology

The website builder, booking tool, analytics and plugins behind a homepage, and what’s out of date.

Reads the homepageWindows appFree

  • About 130 technologies, each with the clue it was found by
  • Flags: no HTTPS, an old analytics tag, old jQuery, PHP or WordPress
  • No mobile viewport, an old copyright year
  • Only identifying headers and cookie names are read, never their values

Know what a site runs on before the first call.

Published contacts

The phone, email, form and social links a business publishes on its own website, with the page each one is on.

Reads the business’s siteWindows appFree

  • The homepage, its sitemap and up to four contact, about or team pages
  • Role or personal address, shown on the page or only in its code
  • “Do not solicit” statements, flagged next to each address
  • Save each address with its page and the date, as a note on the business

Never guesses addresses, never decodes addresses a site hides from robots, never searches other websites.

Site history

When a website was first saved by the Internet Archive and when its homepage last changed.

Internet ArchiveFree

  • First and last saved copies
  • The last time the content changed
  • Changes per year
  • A link to each year’s last copy

A homepage untouched since 2019 is a good reason to call.

Create

Make something

Three makers for the leave-behind: a QR code, a colour kit, a social post.

QR codes

QR codes for a website, a phone number, an email or Wi-Fi, in the business’s colours, as PNG or SVG.

On your computerFree

  • Website, phone, email, Wi-Fi or text
  • Square, rounded or dot styles
  • Colours with a contrast warning, and a logo in the middle
  • PNG or SVG; the Wi-Fi password is never saved

A leave-behind for the counter, made in a minute.

Colour kit

A business’s colours from its logo or its website, with contrast checks and ready-to-paste CSS.

On your computerFree

  • From a logo file or the website’s own styles
  • Contrast for white and black text
  • A darker shade that passes for buttons
  • CSS variables, and “use for my reports”

Mockups and reports in the client’s own colours.

Post maker

Social posts for announcements, offers, hours and events, sized for Instagram, Stories and Facebook.

On your computerFree

  • Announcement, offer, hours, event and tip layouts
  • Square, portrait, Story and link sizes
  • Brand, light or dark, with a logo and photo
  • PNG export; starter text is placeholder copy to replace

A ready post to attach to the first email.

How they stay honest

Facts you can show a business

Free services, no keys

Google PageSpeed, public DNS and the Internet Archive are free to use. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay.

Real businesses only

The business picker lists real OpenStreetMap businesses. Sample businesses have invented websites, so they are never checked.

What it found, nothing more

Every pitch line is built only from what a check found, and every finding keeps its source: a page, a record or a clue.
