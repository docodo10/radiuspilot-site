Try the real app, on sample data

This is RadiusPilot itself, running in your browser on a sample workspace. Every business, number and message in it is invented.

Open RadiusPilot on your PC

RadiusPilot is a Windows app, made for a big screen. Send this page to your computer to try the live demo.

Click “Try it live” to use the app. Move the pointer out of the window to scroll the page again.

The live app opens on screens 900 px wide and more. On a smaller window you see a picture of it.

In the demo

Six screens to open

The rest of the app stays in the Windows app, where it can use your computer.

  • HomeQuick links, recents and sticky notes
  • TodayYour plan, tasks, summary and activity
  • ScopeScanThe sample scans and their results
  • ScopeMapEvery business on the map
  • BusinessesEvery listing with its score, and the best opportunities
  • AnalyticsReplies, meetings and revenue

In the Windows app only

  • New scans
  • Inbox, automations and sequences
  • AI Builder and the website editor
  • The tools
  • Settings and plans

Sample data

The demo’s workspace, its businesses, their scores, emails and revenue are all invented, and they reset each time the demo opens. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere. Real scans use OpenStreetMap, in the Windows app.
