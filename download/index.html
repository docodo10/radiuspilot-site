Install

Up and running in minutes

  1. 1

    Download the installer

    From this page. There’s nothing to sign up for first.

  2. 2

    Run it

    The app isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

  3. 3

    Open RadiusPilot

    Create your account on this computer, or look around the sample workspace first.

  4. 4

    Run your free real scan

    Pick a place, a radius and a few kinds of businesses, and see who needs you.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An internet connection for scans and website checks
  • To send email from the app: your mailbox and an app password

Not tried on Windows yet
