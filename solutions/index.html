Web design studios

Pitch with a draft in hand

Find the businesses with no website or a weak one, and show them a better one before the first call.

  1. 1

    Scan the categories you build for

    Dentists, restaurants, salons or contractors near your office.

  2. 2

    Sort by what’s missing

    Best opportunities puts “No website”, “No mobile layout” and “No HTTPS” first.

  3. 3

    Check the current site

    Run audit grades it A to F: speed, SEO and security, email records, the builder behind it, its history.

  4. 4

    Make a draft

    AI Builder drafts a homepage from the business’s facts. Edit it, then export it as HTML.

  5. 5

    Send it from your mailbox

    An intro email, a call three days later, a follow-up. The sequence stops when they reply.

ScopeScan, Website audit, Site technology, AI Builder and Sequences

Local SEO agencies

Fix the gaps, street by street

Spot the businesses missing hours, a phone or a working site, then bring Google’s own numbers.

  1. 1

    Scan a neighbourhood

    Up to 500 real businesses per scan, with up to 300 homepages checked in the Windows app.

  2. 2

    Find the listing gaps

    No opening hours, no phone, no street address: each one shows beside the score.

  3. 3

    Plan it on ScopeMap

    Every business on the map, coloured by opportunity, one street at a time.

  4. 4

    Bring Google’s numbers

    Site speed runs Google PageSpeed, with its SEO and accessibility scores.

  5. 5

    Send a report

    A market report as PDF, white-labelled with your name and colour on Agency.

Ads and social agencies

Turn a scan into a pipeline

Score the leads, send the sequence, see who replied.

  1. 1

    Scan the businesses that advertise

    Gyms, restaurants, real estate, clinics: whoever lives on local customers.

  2. 2

    Move them through the pipeline

    From New lead to Contacted, Proposal sent and Won, a task for every next step.

  3. 3

    Make something for them

    Post maker sizes a post for Instagram, Stories or Facebook in their colours.

  4. 4

    Follow up on a schedule

    Sequences of emails, calls and tasks. Automations take the routine steps.

  5. 5

    See what works

    Reply rate, meetings booked and the best time to send, over 7, 30 or 90 days.

Pipeline, Post maker, Brand kit and Analytics
