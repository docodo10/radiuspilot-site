Web design studios

Find the businesses with no website or a weak one, and show them a draft before the first call.

  1. 1

    Scan the categories you build for

    Dentists, restaurants, salons or contractors within a few kilometres of your office.

  2. 2

    Sort by what’s missing

    Best opportunities puts “No website”, “No mobile layout” and “No HTTPS” at the top.

  3. 3

    Check the current site

    Run diagnosis grades it from A to F with Google’s speed scores on a phone, its email records, the builder behind it and its history.

  4. 4

    Make a draft

    AI Builder drafts a homepage from the business’s facts. Edit site to make it yours, then export it as HTML.

  5. 5

    Send it from your mailbox

    An intro email, a call three days later, a follow-up: the sequence stops when they reply.

  • Site speed
  • Site technology
  • AI Builder
  • Sequences

Local SEO agencies

Spot the businesses missing hours, a phone or a working site, street by street.

  1. 1

    Scan a neighbourhood

    Up to 500 real businesses per scan, each with its listing and its homepage checked.

  2. 2

    Find the listing gaps

    No opening hours, no phone, no title or description: each one shows next to the score.

  3. 3

    See them on ScopeMap

    Every business on the map, coloured by opportunity, so you can plan a street at a time.

  4. 4

    Bring Google’s numbers

    Site speed runs Google PageSpeed, with its SEO and accessibility scores and its list of fixes.

  5. 5

    Send a report

    A market report as PDF, white-labelled with your name and colour on the Agency plan.

  • ScopeMap
  • Site speed
  • Site history
  • Reports

Ads and social agencies

Turn a scan into a pipeline: score the leads, send the sequence, see who replied.

  1. 1

    Scan the businesses that advertise

    Gyms, restaurants, real estate, clinics: whoever lives on local customers.

  2. 2

    Move them through the pipeline

    From New lead to Contacted, Proposal sent and Won, with tasks for every next step.

  3. 3

    Make something for them

    Post maker sizes a post for Instagram, Stories or Facebook in their colours, from Colour kit.

  4. 4

    Follow up on a schedule

    Sequences of emails, calls and tasks, and Today lines up what’s due. Automations can do the routine steps.

  5. 5

    See what works

    Reply rate, meetings booked and the best time to send, over 7, 30 or 90 days.

Works with

  • Pipeline
  • Post maker
  • Colour kit
  • Analytics

Freelance marketers

One seat, the free plan and a real scan to start. Upgrade the month a client signs.

  1. 1

    Start on the free plan

    One real scan as a preview, five sample scans a month and every tool, for one seat.

    Use the tools on real businesses

    Site speed, Email health and Site history work on any address you type.

  3. 3

    Bring the clients you have

    Import them from a CSV file: Excel, Google Sheets or your CRM’s export.

    With your address and an unsubscribe line on every outreach email, and a daily limit.

  5. 5

    Upgrade when it pays
