Terms of use

The terms for using the RadiusPilot website and app.

Agreeing to these terms

By using this website or the RadiusPilot app, you agree to these terms. If you use RadiusPilot for a business, you agree on its behalf. RadiusPilot is published by the legal name of the business.

What RadiusPilot is

RadiusPilot is a Windows app that finds local businesses from OpenStreetMap, checks their websites, scores them and helps you contact them. It is being prepared for its first release, and features may change.

Sample data in the app and on this site is invented and labelled as such. It isn’t information about real businesses.

Your account

Your account lives on your computer. Keep your password, your backup codes and your Windows account secure; you’re responsible for what is done with your account.

Plans and payments

Payments aren’t live. Choosing a plan in the app today is simulated and charges nothing. Prices shown may change. Before paid plans open, these terms will be updated with how billing, renewals, cancellations and refunds work.

Using RadiusPilot fairly

You agree to:

  • Follow the anti-spam and privacy laws that apply to your messages, such as CASL in Canada and CAN-SPAM in the United States
  • Honour every opt-out, and respect any “do not solicit” statement a business publishes
  • Not use RadiusPilot to collect email addresses in bulk, to guess addresses, or to build profiles of people
  • Not use RadiusPilot to harass anyone, or to send misleading messages
  • Not try to get around the app’s limits, such as the daily sending limit or the opt-out list

Other people’s data and services

Business listings come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License. If you share that data, keep its credit. The tools use Google PageSpeed, public DNS services and the Internet Archive, under their own terms. If you set up AI, it runs on your own OpenAI or ChatGPT account, under OpenAI’s terms.

Drafts and generated content

Websites, decks, reports and emails drafted by the app, including text written by AI, are starting points. Read and check them before you send or publish them; you’re responsible for what you send.

RadiusPilot is provided as it is, without any warranty that it will be free of errors or always available. Scores and checks are based on the data found and can be wrong. Limitation of liability, to be written with a lawyer.
