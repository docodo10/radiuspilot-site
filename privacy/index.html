Privacy

Privacy policy

How Pitchable handles personal information: on this website, in your account on it, and in the Windows app.

DraftLast updated October 27, 2026

A draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer

It describes what Pitchable does today. A lawyer still has to review it for Québec’s Law 25, PIPEDA and CASL, and parts highlighted like this are still to be filled in. It will be final before launch.

In short

  • The Windows app keeps your work on your computer. It doesn’t send it to us.
  • This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising.
  • An account here stores your sign-in, profile, support tickets and account activity, in Canada.
  • You sign in with your Google account. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, never your password.
  • Signed in, you can export your data or delete your account yourself.

Who we are

Pitchable is published by the legal name of the business, its postal address. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is name and title, reachable at email address.

What this policy covers

This website, the account you can create on it, and the Pitchable app for Windows. It doesn’t cover the websites and services the app connects to, which have their own policies.

This website

  • It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • It’s hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, for security, under the GitHub privacy statement.
  • The live demo keeps its demo workspace in your browser’s storage, on your device. It holds no personal information about you. Clear this site’s data in your browser to remove it.
  • When you sign in, your session is kept in your browser’s storage until you sign out or it expires.

Your account on this website

An account is optional. You need one to follow support tickets and manage your sign-in on this website. It’s separate from the account in the Windows app.

Why

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To answer your support tickets
  • To limit abuse, such as too many sign-in attempts or messages in a short time
  • To send you news by email, only if you said yes. Change that in Settings at any time

Where

With Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in Canada.

Signing in with Google

When you choose Continue with Google, Google asks you to confirm, then shares your name, email address and profile picture with us: its basic profile, nothing more. We never see your Google password, and we get no access to your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account. Google handles that step under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove Pitchable from your Google account at any time, in its security settings under third-party connections. That doesn’t delete your Pitchable account: use Delete my account for that.

How long

  • Your sign-in, profile, tickets, messages and account activity: until you delete your account. Copies can remain in our host’s backups until they’re replaced
  • Sign-in records: how long sign-in records are kept
  • Rate-limit counts: deleted after 3 days

Who can see it

  • You, once signed in. Row-level security on our database keeps other accounts out of your data
  • Pitchable staff, to answer support tickets
  • Our service providers, only to run the service: Supabase and, when the bot check is on, Cloudflare. Google knows when you use it to sign in to Pitchable

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

The public key in our pages

Our pages include the server’s public “anon” key. That’s by design: it only lets the page reach our server. Row-level security on the server decides what each signed-in person can read and change, and no table can be read without signing in.

The bot check

Public forms, such as sign-in and contact, can use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from scripts. It’s off until we turn it on. When it’s on, Cloudflare receives your IP address and technical details about your browser for that check, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The contact form

The contact form on the Support page asks for your name, your email, a topic and your message. We keep them with the time you sent them and a salted hash of your IP address, never the address itself. The hash only lets us limit how often one connection can write, and it’s removed after 30 days.

Pitchable staff read contact requests to answer them. They’re deleted 12 months after they’re sent.

The app

The app stores your account, your workspaces and your settings on your computer, and doesn’t send them to us. Your account password is kept as a salted hash. Your mailbox password, and your ChatGPT sign-in or OpenAI API key if you set up AI, are encrypted by Windows for your Windows account.

The app has no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.

Information about businesses and the people who run them

Real scans list businesses from public, openly licensed listings, through the services named above. The Published contacts tool shows only what a business publishes on its own website, one business at a time and at your request, with the page each item comes from. It doesn’t guess addresses or decode hidden ones. It keeps only an address you choose to use as a business’s email, with the page it came from.

Businesses, prospects and clients you import from your own CSV file stay on your computer. You confirm that you’re allowed to email the addresses in it, or leave them out.

You decide what you do with that information, and you’re responsible for following the law when you contact anyone, including Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL) and private-sector privacy laws.

Email

When you connect your mailbox in the app, your email travels between the app and your mail provider. We don’t see it. Every outreach email carries your mailing address and a way to opt out, and the addresses on your opt-out list are blocked.

Workspace sync, later

Syncing the app’s workspaces with your account is planned. Before any workspace data is stored on our servers, this policy will say what is stored, why, for how long and who can see it, and you’ll be asked to agree.

Your rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to withdraw your consent, to have it deleted, or to receive it in a structured, commonly used format.

Signed in, you can do most of this yourself: correct your profile in Settings, download your data with Export my data, and remove everything with Delete my account.

For anything else, send a privacy request from the Support page, or write to email address. We’ll answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Pitchable is a tool for businesses and isn’t meant for children.

Changes to this policy

We’ll post any change on this page with its date, and say so in the changelog when it matters.

Questions about this page?

Write to us from the Support page. Privacy requests go there too.
