Changelog

What’s new in Pitchable

Every change worth knowing, newest first, and what comes next.

Follow the feedComing next

Pitchable 1.0 isn’t out yet

A test version is on the download page. Until the full release, this page lists the work done so far.

October 28, 2026

  1. Improved

    RadiusPilot is now Pitchable

    Same app, same P logo, a new name for what it helps you do: find the local businesses worth pitching, and pitch them with facts.

    • Your workspaces, settings and sign-ins carry over: on its first start, the app moves its data folder to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pitchable
    • Backups and CSV exports made under the old name still import
    • This website keeps its address for now

October 26, 2026

  1. New

    A test version to download

    Pitchable for Windows can be downloaded as a test version: a zip with the app, nothing to install.

    • It hasn’t run on a real Windows computer yet, and it isn’t code-signed
    • Your workspaces stay on your PC
    Go to the download page

  2. New

    AI writing with your own account

    In the Windows app, Pitchable can write with Codex on your PC, your ChatGPT account or an OpenAI API key. Without one, it keeps using its templates.

    • Emails, website copy, edits in the website editor, automation steps and market report text
    • Everything it writes is labelled AI-generated, and nothing is sent without you
    • A daily limit for the analysis it runs in the background
    • Report figures stay Pitchable’s own: the AI’s text has no numbers in it

  3. New

    A website editor for AI Builder

    Open an AI Builder site on a canvas and edit it like a design tool.

    • Layers, blocks, an inspector and three screen sizes
    • Components you reuse across pages, with their own text on each copy
    • An image library for your photos and logos
    • Alt to measure, align and distribute, hover and pressed states
    • Export the site as HTML

  4. New

    Automations

    Workflows that run on their own: a trigger, then the steps you choose.

    • Start from a template, or write the steps as a sentence
    • Schedules, conditions, and approvals before anything is sent
    • Sequences now live here

  5. New

    One-click diagnosis and CSV import

    Check a business’s website in one click, and bring in the businesses you already have.

    • Diagnosis: speed, email health, technology and history, graded A to F, with lines for your pitch
    • Import businesses, prospects and clients from a CSV file, from Excel, Google Sheets or your CRM
    • Imported businesses get no made-up scores

  6. Improved

    A simpler menu and a new Analytics

    The sidebar went from about 30 entries to 18, and the app starts faster.

    • Today holds your plan, tasks, summary and activity; Inbox holds mail and messages
    • Businesses holds the best opportunities, and Reports moved into AI Builder
    • Analytics: five numbers against the previous period, a daily or weekly chart, and highlights
    • Pages load when you open them, so the first screen shows sooner

  7. Improved

    No storage limit, and Tools in six languages

    Bigger workspaces, backups, and Tools pages in every interface language.

    • Workspaces are saved as files on your PC, past the browser’s 5 MB limit
    • Back up and restore them in Settings, then Data
    • The Tools pages in French, Spanish, German, Portuguese and Italian

October 25, 2026

  1. New

    A bigger website

    The website grew from one page to a full site.

    • Pages for the product, the tools, solutions, pricing with a full comparison, and this changelog
    • A help center with step-by-step guides
    • A live demo page that runs the real app in the browser, on a computer
    • Security, privacy and terms pages
    Open the Help center

  2. New

    Published contacts

    The eighth tool: the phone, email, form and social links a business publishes on its own website, with the page each one is on.

    • Reads the homepage and up to five contact, about or team pages of the same site
    • Marks role and personal-looking addresses, and pages that say they don’t want unsolicited messages
    • Addresses a site hides from robots aren’t decoded: you get the page to open instead
    • “Use as business email” keeps the address with the page it came from
    • A “Before you write” reminder about Canada’s anti-spam law
    About Published contacts

  3. Fixed

    The unsubscribe line is always on

    Every outreach email now carries your mailing address and an unsubscribe line. The setting that could switch it off is gone.

    • Client emails are left as they are
    • Templates with their own unsubscribe link keep it instead

  4. New

    Seven tools

    A Tools section in the sidebar, on every plan, for checking a business’s website and making something for it.

    • Site speed, with Google PageSpeed
    • Email health: MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC
    • Site technology: about 130 technologies, with what’s out of date
    • Site history, from the Internet Archive
    • QR codes, Colour kit and Post maker, made on your computer
    See the tools

  5. Improved

    A new sign-in, first run and home

    The app got a new frame and a calmer home screen.

    • Email-first sign-in, with two-step verification and backup codes
    • A five-step first run with a live preview of your workspace
    • A new sidebar with sections that fold, and a breadcrumb bar on every page
    • Home with quick links, recents and sticky notes; Your work with leads, scans, reports and activity

  6. New

    The website, with the real app inside

    This website went live, with the app itself running at the top of the home page.

    • Light and dark, sized for laptops, tablets and phones
    • The live preview opens on the home screen and keeps the main screens one click away
    Open the live demo

  7. New

    Pitchable 1.0

    The first version, in one Windows app.

    • Real scans with no API key, plus demo markets to try every screen
    • Opportunity scores from the facts found, ScopeMap, businesses, opportunities and analytics
    • Mail from your own mailbox, sequences, an email builder, offers and proposals
    • AI Builder drafts, a pipeline, tasks, reports and clients
    • Six interface languages, light and dark
    Tour the product
